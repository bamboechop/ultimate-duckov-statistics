# Explosive barrel attribution

Duckov 2.3.30 can execute a breakable's explosion synchronously inside the projectile that destroyed it. UDS now observes `Breakable.OnDead(DamageInfo)` and gives explosive breakables their own combat scope. Player credit follows the triggering actor, while the damage source is `duckov:weapon:explosive-barrel`, with no ammunition or headshot attribution. The kill feed uses an explosion pictogram and the localized barrel name. Encounter history records the `barrel-explosion` mechanism.

Explosion damage cannot inherit headshot evidence. An explosion without a trusted barrel scope also cannot borrow an enclosing projectile's weapon, ammunition, or accuracy hit unless its native weapon identifies that same gun. Genuine gun explosions retain their own source. Missing or conflicting barrel hooks report degraded capability independently of direct projectile tracking.

Regression coverage includes First Person Camera enabled and disabled, multiple victims, chained NPC explosions, exceptional scope cleanup, foreign hook conflicts, nonexplosive breakables, and genuine gun explosions. The installed native contract probe checks the exact breakable method and explosion flag.

The installed FPC build also patches `Breakable.OnDead` with `FirstPersonCamera.Patches.Breakable_OnDead_ExplosionCameraShake_Patch.Prefix`. Its inspected callback only notifies camera/environment effects, without changing damage or suppressing the original method. The initial correction recognized this exact prefix through binary-identity and patch-order checks. The broader record-by-default policy now permits foreign observation patches while verifying UDS callbacks; private FPC headshot interpretation still retains its specialized verification. Without this compatibility entry, the native barrel hook was rejected and the feed never received the barrel source ID; changing icon rendering alone could not repair that path. The feed now draws its barrel burst directly with opaque UI shapes. Native activation and live visual confirmation remain distinct from automated checks.

On 2026-10-25, three user-confirmed barrel kills in slot 6 were corrected from their triggering guns to barrels. Total kills and damage were preserved. Three false headshots and headshot final blows were removed. One isolated false accuracy hit was removed; the other two historical hit counts were preserved because retained records cannot isolate projectile completion identity. The tutorial correction retains its previously recorded surrogate provenance. Slot 4 and native game saves were untouched.

Local repair evidence, the original profile backup, and verified readback are under `artifacts/barrel-fix-20261025/final/`. The complete validation passed 2,583 main tests and 218 shell tests in each of Debug and Release, the installed native contract probe, and package validation.
